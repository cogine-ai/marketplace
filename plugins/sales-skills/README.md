# Sales Skills

Sales Skills is a focused, cross-platform toolkit for Codex and Claude Code.
It covers the path from founder-led discovery and first customers through
prospecting, outreach, sales calls, enterprise execution, and pipeline review.

## Included skills

### Founder and motion design — 3

- founder-sales
- first-b2b-customers
- enterprise-sales-motion

### Prospecting and outreach — 3

- prospecting
- account-research
- cold-email

### Calls and enablement — 3

- call-prep
- call-review
- sales-enablement

### Account and revenue execution — 3

- enterprise-account-planning
- pipeline-review
- plg-sales-integration

## Design choices

- Founder sales and first B2B customers remain separate because they answer
  different questions: how the founder sells and how the first accounts are won.
- Account research, enterprise account planning, and pipeline review operate at
  different levels: evidence gathering, one strategic deal, and the portfolio.
- call-review keeps the useful summary and follow-up workflow while loading
  coaching only when requested.
- plg-sales-integration is intentionally shared with Growth & GTM because PQL
  design and sales-assisted expansion belong to both roles.
- Tool-specific integrations are optional. No skill requires a particular CRM,
  enrichment vendor, meeting recorder, or email provider.
