# Measure Format Choices by Platform and Time

Content format is a testable choice. A historic share from one platform or vendor study does not establish the best format for the current audience.

## Build a comparable sample

1. Fix a representative query set and intended audience/locale. Record the platform, model when visible, date, search mode, and relevant session settings.
2. Run each query 3–5 times per platform as an initial sample. Keep the wording and test conditions comparable; record personalization or prior conversation that could affect the result.
3. Save each valid answer and cited URLs. Separately label retrieved evidence when observable, citations, brand mentions, recommendations, and downstream visits/conversions. Retrieval cannot be inferred from a missing citation alone.
4. Report counts and denominators: “cited in 3 of 5 valid runs; n=5”. Keep errors, blocked requests, and unavailable surfaces outside the valid-answer denominator and report them explicitly.
5. Repeat at the intended observation interval. A changed rate in a small sample is a reason to investigate, not proof of a ranking update or causal lift.

## Choose and reassess a format

Classify observed sources as product/docs/pricing pages, comparisons, guides, original data, video text, or other relevant formats. Evaluate the answer's job and the source's evidence and accessibility before recommending a format. Test the selected approach against qualified discovery and business outcomes, and keep existing useful content unless evidence supports changing it.

## Query fan-out evidence

When actual retrieval queries are exposed by available browsing or tool traces, record their exact text and context. Otherwise describe proposed fan-out queries as hypotheses. Do not rely on a particular DevTools field or claim to have observed background queries that were not available.
