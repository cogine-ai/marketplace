# Positioning and Consensus in AI Answers

Use this for repositioning, a merger or rebrand, segment prompts, or inconsistent
brand descriptions. Upstream practitioner accounts suggest these changes can
move slowly; they are hypotheses to test, not a guaranteed timeline or ranking
mechanism.

## Before changing the narrative

1. Record comparable prompts and results for both the current and intended
   segments. Separate citations, mentions, recommendations, and recommendation
   framing using [format-volatility.md](format-volatility.md).
2. Inventory the About/product pages, review profiles, company pages, partner
   listings, and independent coverage. Record source and check date.
3. Reconcile truthful category, audience, and differentiator descriptions.
   Consistency should reflect the actual product and buyers, not manufactured
   consensus. Draft profile changes; publish only when authorized.
4. Stage the transition and preserve useful old-segment pages while measuring
   the new segment. Do not erase proven demand merely because copy changed.

## Work from observed retrieval

When tool traces expose actual fan-out queries, save their exact text and
context. Otherwise label proposed background queries as hypotheses. Prioritize
the domains appearing in the user's own observations rather than assuming a
particular DevTools field or vendor's list applies to every category.

Build industry or segment pages only for buyers actually served, with specific
workflows and supported proof; see [content-types.md](content-types.md) and the
bundled [programmatic-seo.md](programmatic-seo.md). Use `product-marketing` for
positioning and `customer-research` for authentic buyer evidence. No separate
directory-submissions or public-relations skill is required.

## Repair repeated negative claims

Trace the cited sources and distinguish accurate limitations from stale or
unsupported claims. Address real weaknesses with documented product changes,
implementation evidence, and realistic timelines. Draft an accuracy correction
to a publisher only when supported; sending it requires authorization. Do not
ask for a true negative to be hidden or replaced merely to improve positioning.
Avoid invented awards, fake independent reviews, and fabricated customer proof.

For useful category guides, compare options honestly and identify where each
fits. Ranking a leader first is a possible editorial choice, not a retrieval
guarantee. Measure recommendation outcomes separately from citations; see
[citations-vs-recommendations.md](citations-vs-recommendations.md).
