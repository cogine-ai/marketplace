# Video Text Layers for Discovery

For queries where video is relevant, inspect the text a search system or agent can actually access. Do not assume all models can or cannot consume the footage.

- **Transcript:** provide an accurate account of the spoken explanation, with correct names, terms, and context. Preserve the meaning of the source.
- **Captions:** correct transcription errors, punctuation, and product names; do not claim captions are present until verified.
- **Title:** name the actual question or outcome the video answers.
- **Chapters:** use useful timestamps and labels for the subquestions covered; verify they lead to the right passage.
- **Description:** summarize the answer and link to relevant first-party sources or the associated product/docs page.
- **Pinned summary, if used:** keep it consistent with the video and point to supporting detail. Drafting the summary does not authorize posting it.

Verify the text layers on the published page when available, or mark them as planned if the video is still a draft. Compare observed retrieval/citations and qualified engagement with [the sampling method](format-volatility.md), not a promised platform-wide citation uplift.
